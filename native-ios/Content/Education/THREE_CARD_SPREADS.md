# Three-card spread methodology

The app offers four ways to assign meaning to three positions before shuffling:

1. `Past · Present · Possible Future`
2. `Situation · Challenge · Advice`
3. `You · The other person · Connection`
4. `Open reading` (`Card 1 · Card 2 · Card 3`)

These are modern, widely used reading patterns rather than a claim that one historical authority defined the only correct three-card method. Contemporary teaching references list close equivalents including Past/Present/Future, Current situation/Obstacle/Advice, and You/The other person/The relationship:

- [Biddy Tarot — 25 Easy Three-Card Tarot Spreads](https://biddytarot.com/blog/easy-three-card-tarot-spreads/)
- [Labyrinthos — 3 Card Tarot Spreads](https://labyrinthos.co/blogs/learn-tarot-with-labyrinthos-academy/3-card-tarot-spreads-simple-tarot-spreads-organized-by-layout?page=2)

Product adaptations are deliberate:

- `Possible Future` avoids presenting the third position as a fixed prediction.
- `Challenge` is broader and less adversarial than treating every difficulty as an obstacle.
- `Connection` includes relationships beyond romance and does not assert facts about another person's private thoughts.
- `Open reading` preserves the user's ability to define roles or read the cards as a sequence.

The selected spread persists by a language-neutral ID. Labels localize to English or Spanish, while deck order, card IDs, draws, and reveal states remain unchanged.
